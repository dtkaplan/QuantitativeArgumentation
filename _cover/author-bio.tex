{\bf Daniel Kaplan} is  DeWitt Wallace Professor of Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science at Macalester College where he directs the Applied Mathematics and Statistics program.  A winner of Macalester's {\em Excellence in Teaching} award, he earned a B.A. in physics from Swarthmore College and a Ph.D. in biomedical physics from Harvard University.
